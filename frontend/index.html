    Navigate

    Left-drag rotate · Right-drag pan · Scroll zoom

    T top view · F fit model · 1 2 3 photo / height / points

    Measure

    Choose a tool, then click on the model. Paths and polygons: Enter or double-click to finish, ⌫ to undo a point, Esc to cancel.

    Tools

    • I Point lat/lon, MGRS, UTM, height
    • D Distance ground and 3D length of a path
    • H Height ground to top of a structure
    • A Area flat and surface area, perimeter
    • V Volume material above / below a base fitted to the outline
    • P Profile elevation chart, climb and slope
    • L Sight line can one position see another?
    • N Note label a location

    Distances, heights, areas and volumes are measured inside the model and are consistent with each other. Absolute positions depend on the drone GPS; see Model quality for the expected offset.
